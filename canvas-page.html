PROTECT THE SEARCH

Confidentiality & Conflicts of Interest

Protect candidate information, committee deliberations, and the integrity of the hiring process.

Serving on a search committee gives you access to information that must remain confidential. Candidate materials, committee discussions, and decisions about who advances should stay within the official search process.

Keep the search inside the search

Confidentiality applies to both candidate information and the committee's deliberations.

Keep private

  • Applicant materials
  • Candidate status
  • Committee discussions
  • Committee deliberations and recommendations

Handle materials securely

  • Do not save application materials to personal or work computers.
  • Do not discuss applicants outside official committee meetings.
  • Meet where conversations cannot be easily overheard.

CONFLICT OF INTEREST

Recognize a connection? Stop and report it.

If an applicant has a family, partner, or current relationship connection to a search committee member, the conflict should be reported to the Search Chair and Human Resources. The committee member should not continue evaluating that applicant.
